## The four canonical verticals

### 1. Treasury Rebalance

A DAO with $10M+ in treasury rebalances quarterly. With aggregators it pays 10–30 bps in spread; with Filler SDK, the DAO's solver fills the DAO's intents — spread stays internal. **Hero example**, ships in [`examples/treasury-rebalance`](https://github.com/filler-sdk/filler-sdk/tree/main/examples/treasury-rebalance).

### 2. Hook-Specific

Every new v4 hook is a 6–12 month window without institutional solver coverage. Hook authors run their own Filler-based solver to bootstrap liquidity for users of their hook.

### 3. LVR-Aware

Solvers that redistribute LVR (loss-versus-rebalancing) back to LPs as a core mechanic. Distinct from arbitrage — the strategy explicitly compensates passive LPs.

### 4. Atomic Compound

Fills composed with other on-chain actions in one tx — yield deposits, hedge legs, governance actions. Removes inter-step inventory risk.

## What we DON'T enable

- Competing with Wintermute / SCP / Propeller on majors. They have CEX-grade pricing pipelines we don't.
